# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis

> Analysis on 9,800 retail transactions across 4 regions, 3 categories, and 4 years (2015–2018).

---

## Overview

Analyzed a retail sales dataset to uncover revenue trends, regional performance gaps, and top-selling categories. The project covers data preparation, YoY growth analysis, and seasonal demand patterns.

---

## Key Findings

| # | Finding |
|---|---------|
| 1 | **Technology is the top revenue category** — leading Office Supplies and Furniture |
| 2 | **West region leads all regions** in total sales across 4 years |
| 3 | **Q4 consistently peaks** — seasonal demand drives the highest monthly sales |
| 4 | **Sales grew YoY** from 2015 to 2018 across all regions and categories |

---

## Analysis Structure

| Step | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1 — Data Preparation | Parse dates, extract time features, verify missing values |
| 2 — YoY Sales | Revenue growth year over year |
| 3 — Regional Analysis | Sales breakdown across West, East, Central, South |
| 4 — Category Analysis | Revenue by Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology |
| 5 — Monthly Trends | Seasonal patterns across 4 years |
| 6 — Top Sub-Categories | Best performing product sub-categories |
